How to play

There is no goal and no timer. Explore, climb, and see what the town has.

Move

WASD or arrow keys. Hold shift to run.

Jump

space. Cars, benches, bins and window sills are all fair game. Jump at a wall and keep pushing to scramble up.

Climb

Hold E at a wall, tree, lamp post or chimney. Stairs and low steps you just walk up.

Everything else

Walk up to people to say hello. Walk over a shiny to pick it up. H hats, M map, N nap, F fullscreen.

Phone: play in landscape. Left thumb joystick to move, right thumb for run, climb and jump; drag anywhere else to look. Gamepad: left stick move, right stick look, A jump, B or RB climb, triggers run.

Install by hand

  1. iPhone or iPad: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen.”
  2. Android: open Chrome’s ⋮ menu, then “Install app” or “Add to Home screen.”
  3. Desktop: use the install icon in Chrome or Edge’s address bar, or choose “Install Whiskerfield” in the browser menu.

Open the game once while online and wait for “Offline ready”. After that it runs without a connection. Progress is saved in this browser.
