THE ART OF DOING VERY LITTLE

Follow your whiskers.

There’s no finish line here. Just a soft breeze, interesting corners, and a new view from the top.

Wander your way

W A S D or arrow keys to move. Hold shift for a happy little run.

A little leap

space to jump onto benches, low walls, and ledges. You always land on your feet.

Up is an option

Get close to a wall and hold E to climb. Let go at the top to settle onto the roof.

A fresh perspective

Drag the world to look around. Scroll to zoom. M opens your neighborhood map.

On a phone? Use the paw joystick and the jump, climb, and run buttons. Drag anywhere else to look around. A Bluetooth gamepad also works: left stick move, right stick look, A jump, B/RB climb, triggers run.
