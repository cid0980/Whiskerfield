THE ART OF DOING VERY LITTLE

Follow your whiskers.

There’s no finish line here. Just a soft breeze, interesting corners, and a new view from the top.

Wander your way

W A S D or arrow keys to move. Hold shift to run flat out.

A little leap

space to jump onto cars, benches, pots, and window sills. Jump at a wall and keep pushing to scramble up it.

Up is an option

Hold E by a wall, tree trunk, lamp post, or chimney to climb. Trees have a comfy perch on top.

Say hello

Walk up to a neighbour and they’ll stop for a fuss. M opens the map, N takes a nap, F goes fullscreen.

On a phone, play sideways: the paw joystick moves, the run button latches on and off, and jump and climb sit under your right thumb. Drag anywhere else to look around. A Bluetooth gamepad works too: left stick move, right stick look, A jump, B/RB climb, triggers run, L3 toggles run.
